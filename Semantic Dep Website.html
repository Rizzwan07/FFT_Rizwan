    About Department

    The Department of Computer Science provides students with a strong foundation in computer science and modern technologies.

    Students learn programming, databases, networking, artificial intelligence, data science and other emerging areas of technology.

    The department also encourages students to participate in workshops, hackathons, seminars and technical competitions.

    Our Vision

    To create skilled, innovative and responsible professionals who can use technology to solve real-world problems.
